BAREGPS ROUTE MAKER - IOS
Version 0.1.0 build 1

This is an Xcode source bundle, not an installable IPA or TestFlight download. Install Node dependencies, open ios/App/App.xcodeproj in Xcode, choose your development team and connected iPhone, and build/run. The bundled web assets are ready.

Maps and terrain require internet. Route lines do not automatically follow paths.
