BAREGPS ROUTE MAKER - ANDROID
Version 0.1.4 build 8

Install the signed APK as an update, or unzip the Android bundle and open its APK. Keep your GPX originals. No account is required.

Native distribution targets Android phones (small/normal screen classes). Tablets should use https://appsbyonzy.dev/tools/baregps-route-maker/ . The manifest filters store distribution; it does not block manual APK sideloading.

Maps and terrain require internet. Route lines do not automatically follow paths.
